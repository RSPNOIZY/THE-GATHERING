### The Five Epochs of Music

The history of the music industry reveals a clear pattern of transformation, with the "product" evolving in each epoch:

| Epoch | Time Frame | Primary Product | Value Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I. The Foundations | 1400s–1900s | Notation (Sheet Music) | Physical Sale of Paper / Printing Monopoly |
| II. The Recording Era | 1920s–1990s | Performance (Records/CDs) | Physical Sale of an Object |
| III. The Digital Disruption | 2000s | File (MP3s) | File Ownership (99¢) or Piracy |
| IV. The Streaming Age | 2010s–Present | Access (Rights/Streams) | Licensing of IP (Recurring Micro-Payment) |
| **V. The NOIZY Epoch** | **2026–** | **Protocol & Provenance** | **Consent-as-Code / Inheritable Voice Estate** |

The Streaming Age treats IP as a financial asset to be licensed by financial institutions. The NOIZY model establishes higher ground: enforcing IP via code. Consent-as-Code and NOIZY PROOF bypass the foundational friction of the streaming age — the constant legal battle over rights, licensing, and unauthorized data use.

The final shift: from Selling to Architecting. The history shows power shifting from Printer → Record Label → Tech Platform. The 5th Epoch shift is from Platform to **Protocol Architect**.
