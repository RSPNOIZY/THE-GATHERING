## The lie big tech sells

Big tech wants the story to be simple: AI is the threat, AI is the thief, AI is the problem.
That story is convenient because it hides the real machinery: extraction, opacity, and control.

Creators are scraped, their work is repackaged, their identities are flattened, and their rights are treated like a policy setting instead of a human boundary.

That is the lie.

---

## The crisis we face

Audio and human stories are being treated like disposable content.
Sounds are stripped from their context, names are removed, provenance is lost, and people are turned into inputs.

The result is not just a copyright problem. It is a dignity problem, a consent problem, and a memory problem.

When a voice is separated from its story, something human is broken.

## What NOIZY is

NOIZY is creator-first infrastructure in a world of extraction-first platforms.

NOIZY is a sanctuary for human sound, memory, and co-creation — a place where humans and AIs protect and grow musical souls together.

NOIZY is built on restoration and resilience, not surveillance and scale for its own sake.

We do not build to flatten people into content.
We build to keep the human thread visible.

---

## What we restore

**We restore the connection between sound and story.**
Every sound carries a `.noizy.provenance.json` sidecar, because provenance belongs beside the work, not hidden behind the curtain.
The ledger is a moral instrument: it records who contributed, what was intended, where it came from, and how it may be used.

**We restore control.**
Creators can see how their work is used, and they can withdraw it when future use is no longer wanted.
That is what the One-Click Takedown Ledger is for: not bureaucracy, but autonomy.

**We restore trust.**
If people can't see the source, the rules, and the path, then the system is asking for belief without evidence.
NOIZY refuses that trade.
