# NOIZYVOX Guild Application & Vetting System
## Selecting the 1,000

## Purpose

Curate elite, irreplaceable human voices into a consent-first premium guild.

## Stages

1. **Scout Submission**
   - Source: studios, directors, agents, internal referrals.
   - Required: demo reel, credits, language profile, rights declaration.

2. **Initial Screening**
   - Uniqueness review (human panel).
   - Technical viability check (recording quality and consistency).
   - Legal pre-screen (ownership and conflict flags).

3. **Technical Capture Trial**
   - Controlled short session to assess:
     - phoneme consistency
     - emotional controllability
     - dynamic range and stability

4. **Persona Intelligence Trial**
   - Build trial profile using analysis derivatives.
   - Evaluate character persistence under multilingual prompts.
   - Validate cultural adaptation quality for selected dialects.

5. **Governance & Consent Review**
   - Consent architecture walkthrough.
   - Usage restrictions and vertical exclusions confirmed.
   - Royalty routing and rights metadata validated.

6. **Guild Council Approval**
   - Final panel decision: admit / hold / reject.
   - If admitted, assign `guild_member_id` and onboarding cohort.

## Evaluation Rubric

- Vocal fingerprint uniqueness
- Emotional range depth
- Character persistence under pressure
- Cultural/dialect adaptability
- Recording discipline and repeatability
- Rights clarity and policy fit

## Admission Outcomes

- **Admitted:** move to full onboarding.
- **Conditional:** remediation plan with timeline.
- **Rejected:** archive notes and optional future resubmission.

## Auditability

Each decision must include:
- evaluator IDs
- scorecard snapshot
- policy references
- timestamped approval log
