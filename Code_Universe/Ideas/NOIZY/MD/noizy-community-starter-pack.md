# NOIZY Community Starter Place

Prepared starting place for voice actors to form a strong bond with the NOIZY.AI community.

## Objective

Give every actor a clear first week:

- establish trust and ownership
- produce first working AVA output
- connect with composers and mentors
- publish first controlled voice card

## First 7 Days

1. **Day 1 - Welcome + Covenant**
   - Review Creator Covenant and activate AVA profile.
2. **Day 2 - Capture Pack**
   - Record calm, narrative, dramatic, conversational sets.
3. **Day 3 - Preview + Corrections**
   - Approve/reject outputs and add performance notes.
4. **Day 4 - Community Intro**
   - Join Composer Guild and introduce your voice style.
5. **Day 5 - First Co-Creation**
   - Run one actor + composer mini session in DreamChamber.
6. **Day 6 - Publish Voice Card**
   - Set allowed contexts and publish first licensed card.
7. **Day 7 - Reflect + Mentor Loop**
   - Log progress, plan next capture pass, schedule mentor check-in.

## API Support

- `GET /onboarding/community-starter-pack`
- `POST /onboarding/{ava_slug}/initialize`
- `GET /onboarding/{ava_slug}/progress`
- `POST /onboarding/{ava_slug}/progress`

## Product Rule

Actors should feel guided and empowered from the first session.
Complex infrastructure stays hidden behind NOIZY services.
