# NOIZYKIDZ

**Domain:** `noizykidz.com`
**Status:** 📋 Planned
**Role in universe:** Haptic Music Education for children (deaf / hard-of-hearing) — Robert's personal mission.

## Purpose
Music is not sound alone. It is vibration, pattern, frequency — all of which can be felt.
Make music education accessible to kids who do not hear it the way hearing kids do.

## Core idea
- Haptic playback devices synced to audio.
- Visual + vibrational representation of rhythm, pitch, dynamics.
- Curriculum built with deaf educators, not imposed on them.
