## What is NOIZYARMY?

NOIZYARMY is a global network of human sound-makers building a shared
library of the world's sounds — organized, credited, licensed, and alive
inside NOIZY.

This is not a sample pack marketplace. It's not a stream.  
It is a **protocol** for human creators to share sounds with each other
and with AI in a way that keeps creators in control.

Every sound you submit is credited to you forever.  
You choose the license. You can revoke at any time.  
If your voice goes in, it stays behind a consent gate — always.

We are building the sonic equivalent of a world ethnomusicology archive,
except the creators get paid and the AI isn't in charge.

## Pack Format

Your pack is a folder with this structure:

```
your-pack-name/
  manifest.noizy.json    ← required: who you are, what this is, license
  audio/
    sound-01.wav         ← your audio files
    sound-02.wav
    ...
  preview.mp3            ← optional: 30-second preview mix
```

**The manifest** tells NOIZY who made this, where you're from, and
what the sounds mean. Here is a template:

```json
{
  "schema_version": "noizyarmy-v1",
  "pack_id": "your-name-description-2026",
  "creator": {
    "name": "Your Name or Handle",
    "contact": "@yourhandle or email",
    "region": "west_africa",
    "city": "Lagos"
  },
  "story": "Two or three sentences about what these sounds are and why you made them.",
  "mood_tags": ["ritual", "organic"],
  "style_tags": ["field recording", "percussion"],
  "license": "cc-by-4.0",
  "sounds": [
    {
      "filename": "sound-01.wav",
      "description": "A wooden crate hit with an open palm in Balogun Market.",
      "instrument_family": "found_object",
      "articulation": "struck"
    }
  ],
  "created_at": "2026-06-21T00:00:00Z"
}
```

## Voice packs — extra step

If your pack contains human voice recordings (singing, speaking, breath, vocal textures):

Add this block to your manifest:

```json
"hvs_consent": {
  "actor_name": "Your Name (the voice owner)",
  "consent_granted": true,
  "revocable": true,
  "synthetic_use_allowed": false,
  "notes": "Sample use only. No synthesis without explicit second consent."
}
```

`synthetic_use_allowed: false` means your voice can be used as a sample
(played back, mixed) but NEVER used to train or synthesize a voice model
without you explicitly saying yes again. This is enforced in code,
not just policy.

## What happens to your pack

1. RSP_001 validates it locally (format, SHA256, license)
2. It gets cataloged into NOIZY with your credits intact
3. GABRIEL and LUCY can explore it — finding patterns, pairing sounds,
   suggesting combinations — but always crediting you
4. If NOIZY generates any commercial revenue using your sounds,
   you are owed 75% of that attribution (NOIZY artist split — standard)
5. Your pack gets its own page in the NOIZY AQUARIUM archive
