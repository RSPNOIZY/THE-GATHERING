# CLIENT #001 Manifesto
## RSP_001 · NOIZYFISH · NOIZYVOX

## Declaration

NOIZYVOX is an artist-owned voice intelligence partnership.

The actor is the source, owner, and rights authority.
NOIZY builds the engine, governance, and distribution rails.
Every generated asset is consent-locked, attributable, and monetizable.

## What Client #001 establishes

1. Proven source-voice pathway from capture to deployable library.
2. Rights-first architecture from day one (consent, metadata, usage policy).
3. Premium quality baseline for long-term reprocessing and immersive outputs.
4. Repeatable blueprint for every subsequent world-class voice partner.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. Human performance is the origin of value.
2. Consent is architecture, not legal boilerplate.
3. Ownership remains with the artist.
4. Revenue participation is transparent and auditable.
5. Every output is traceable to source and policy.

## Platform commitment

- Preserve voice identity across technological change.
- Keep archival masters for re-training and future model upgrades.
- Deliver runtime-ready assets for film, game, and interactive media.
- Maintain creator-safe controls for revocation and usage boundaries.

## Partner promise

Your voice. Your rules. Your royalties.
No hidden extraction.
No rights ambiguity.
No expiration on the value of your creative identity.
