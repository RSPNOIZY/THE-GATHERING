## THE CORE THESIS

> NOIZY exists to make artists sovereign inside AI-native culture
> instead of turning them into extractive training material.

**Two sides:**

**Creative:** Some people already hear the finished symphony before any machine can render it. The problem is never imagination. The problem is *translation*. NOIZY's job is to make translation less lossy.

**Economic:** If AI becomes the primary interface for creative work, artists don't just need tools — they need *infrastructure*. Provenance. Consent. Rights. Payout. Archives. Succession.

## THE 7 CANONICAL CLAIMS (The Manifesto)

1. Artists are not datasets.
2. Provenance is not decoration — it is evidence.
3. AI should amplify human intent, not erase it.
4. AI should reduce friction between inner vision and shared artifact.
5. Cultural lineages should remain visible as living sources, not flattened into style packs.
6. Verified human contribution should become a premium class.
7. Creative systems should preserve authorship, consent, privacy, payout, and legacy.
