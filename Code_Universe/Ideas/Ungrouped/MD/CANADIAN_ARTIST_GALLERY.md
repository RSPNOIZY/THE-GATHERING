## The Vision

Every week, the NOIZY.ai homepage features one independent Canadian artist.

Any form of visual creation — painting, digital art, photography, sculpture, installation, mixed media, generative art, illustration, street art, printmaking, textile, ceramics, film, animation. If it's visual and it's theirs and it's human, it belongs here.

Requirement: aligned with NOIZY culture.

---

## What NOIZY Culture Means for This Gallery

**Belongs here:**
- Work that is unmistakably *theirs* (strong identity, not trend-chasing)
- Human-made or human-directed (provenance visible)
- Artists who are building something, not just producing content
- Work with emotional truth in it — joy, grief, memory, fury, tenderness
- Artists from across the full spectrum of Canada (not just Toronto/Vancouver)
- Indigenous artists, immigrant artists, queer artists, artists from the margins
- People who've been overlooked by the gallery system

**Doesn't belong here:**
- Corporate aesthetic (polished-but-empty)
- AI-generated work presented as human-made
- Work without a clear point of view
- Trend-following for its own sake

---

## Weekly Feature Structure

Each week's feature includes:
- Full-width artwork on homepage
- Artist name + short story (in their voice — not press release language)
- Where to find / follow / buy their work
- NOIZY connection: why this artist, why this week
