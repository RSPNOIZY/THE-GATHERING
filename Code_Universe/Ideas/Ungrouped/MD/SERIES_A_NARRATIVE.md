### The 5th Epoch Thesis

The history of the music business is a 600-year pattern: new technology, new power class, same extraction.

| Epoch | Era | Product | Power | Creator's Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I — Notation | 1400s–1920s | Sheet Music | The Printer | Fraction of print |
| II — Recording | 1920s–1990s | The Recording | The Label | 8–15% after recoupment |
| III — Digital | 2000s | The File | Apple/Platform | ~$0.09/download |
| IV — Streaming | 2010s–Present | Access | The Algorithm | $0.003/stream |
| V — Protocol | 2026–2526 | The Inheritable Asset | The Creator | 75% · Code-enforced |

Every prior transition created a new intermediary. NOIZY's transition removes the intermediary permanently by making consent and attribution function at the infrastructure level.

**The TCP/IP analogy — for investors:**

The internet still runs on TCP/IP, a protocol designed in 1974. It was not disrupted by browsers, by smartphones, by social media, or by AI. It was adopted by all of them. Every new technology that moves data relies on TCP/IP.

NOIZY's Consent-as-Code is designed with the same architecture logic. It is not a platform that can be disrupted by the next technology. It is the consent layer that the next technology will need to run on. The future Spotifys, Apples, and Metas will not compete with NOIZY. They will need to license NOIZY's protocol to access the only legally bulletproof, ethically certified creative AI training dataset in existence.

**The Agentic Royalty — a new class of IP income:**

In 1909, the Copyright Act created the mechanical royalty — a legal requirement for payment every time a song is mechanically reproduced. That royalty still flows today, 117 years later.

NOIZY creates the Agentic Royalty — a technical requirement, enforced by code, for payment every time a GABRIEL agent or any licensed AI uses a Guild creator's work. Not quarterly. Not after recoupment. In real time, to the creator's account, forever — including 70 years after the creator's death.
