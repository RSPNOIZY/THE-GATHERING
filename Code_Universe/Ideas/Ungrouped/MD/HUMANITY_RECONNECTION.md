## The Diagnosis

The problem is not that humanity lost depth completely.
The problem is that depth has been **buried under convenience, abstraction, isolation, and commodified culture.**

Five mechanisms of burial:
1. **Digital abstraction** — screens flatten experience; algorithmic feeds pre-digest culture; people consume more than they create
2. **Standardization** — mass culture rewards repeatability; risk and strangeness become niche; local forms collapse into marketable sameness
3. **Educational narrowing** — performance metrics outrank expression; creativity treated as enrichment, not infrastructure
4. **Community fragmentation** — rituals disappear; intergenerational contact weakens; digital substitutes replace embodied gathering
5. **Devaluation of the arts** — without art, people lose regular contact with grief, joy, wonder, tension, and transformation
