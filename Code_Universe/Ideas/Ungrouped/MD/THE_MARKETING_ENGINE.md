### 3. THE ANTI-GENERICA LISTENING TESTS

A monthly experiment. Publicly documented. Results posted live.

**The format:**
- Pair identical musical prompts given to a Guild artist and to an AI generation platform
- Play both, unlabeled, to 100 listeners recruited from different demographics and countries
- Measure: emotional response (self-reported), physiological response (with participant consent), preference, and — most importantly — *which one they felt in their body*
- Publish the results unedited

**Why this works:** The neuroacoustic science is real. Human-origin audio produces different responses. But the science is abstract until you watch 87 out of 100 people correctly identify the human-origin audio without being told which is which — and then watch their faces when they learn they were right.

**The media angle:** This is not a press release. It is a recurring experiment with public data. Music journalists, science journalists, and AI ethics writers will cover the results. The results become the story.
