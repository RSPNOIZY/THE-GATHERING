# NOIZY Voice Actor Onboarding

Goal: Voice actors learn to use NOIZY AI workflows without needing ML engineering.

## Principle

Actors focus on performance and control.  
System handles model training, orchestration, and deployment.

## 7-Step Actor Workflow

1. **Create profile**
   - Set name, handle, tone presets, and allowed use cases.
2. **Consent setup**
   - Confirm training rights and licensing boundaries.
3. **Record capture pack**
   - Calm, narrative, dramatic, and conversational reads.
4. **Review AI preview**
   - Listen to generated samples and mark issues.
5. **Tune performance controls**
   - Pace, dynamics, emotion profile, pronunciation notes.
6. **Publish voice card**
   - Enable only approved contexts and pricing tiers.
7. **Monitor usage**
   - Track receipts, revoke contexts, and update training data.

## What Actors Must Learn

- How to capture clean recordings.
- How to approve or reject generated outputs.
- How to set consent, licensing, and revocation.
- How to manage their voice as a long-term creative asset.

## What Actors Do Not Need To Learn

- Neural network internals.
- PyTorch training operations.
- Infrastructure orchestration.
- Cloud deployment details.

## Training Path (2 Weeks)

### Week 1
- Day 1: Product orientation + consent controls
- Day 2: Recording workflow + quality standards
- Day 3: First voice model preview + correction loop
- Day 4: Emotion and performance tuning
- Day 5: Publish pilot voice card

### Week 2
- Day 6: Licensing and context restrictions
- Day 7: Usage dashboards and receipts
- Day 8: Revocation and safety controls
- Day 9: Collaboration in Composer Guild workflows
- Day 10: Certification as NOIZY-ready voice actor

## Success Metrics

- Time to first approved voice card
- First-pass acceptance rate on generated lines
- Number of active licensed contexts per actor
- Actor satisfaction with control and quality
- Revocation response time for restricted use
