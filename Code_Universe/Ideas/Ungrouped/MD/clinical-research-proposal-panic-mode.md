# Clinical Research Proposal Outline — NOIZYVOX Panic Mode

## Working Title

Biometric-triggered voice + haptic + auditory pacing for acute anxiety regulation: feasibility and safety study.

## Study Type

- Phase 0/1 feasibility
- non-invasive digital intervention
- ethics-reviewed protocol required before any clinical claims

## Objectives

1. Evaluate usability and adherence in real-world sessions.
2. Measure short-window changes in HR/HRV after intervention start.
3. Compare protocol pathways (box, 4-7-8, physiological sigh).

## Endpoints

- primary:
  - intervention completion rate
  - self-reported distress delta
- secondary:
  - HR recovery slope
  - HRV recovery delta
  - escalation frequency

## Inclusion/Exclusion (Draft)

- adults with recurrent anxiety episodes (initial cohort)
- optional neurodiversity subgroup in a separate protocol
- exclude participants needing immediate emergency care pathways

## Risk Controls

- immediate stop control
- no medical replacement language
- escalation guidance for persistent severe symptoms
- adverse-event logging and review

## Data Plan

- de-identified biometric snapshots
- protocol choice + outcome tags
- auditable consent records

## Analysis Plan

- within-subject pre/post comparisons
- protocol-level subgroup analysis
- sensitivity analysis by baseline variability

## Deliverables

- feasibility report
- safety assessment
- protocol revision recommendations
- go/no-go criteria for larger trial
