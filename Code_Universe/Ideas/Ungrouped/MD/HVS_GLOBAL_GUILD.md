## WHAT THIS IS

The Human Voice Signature is a certification mark.

Not a format. Not a file type. Not a plugin.

A **declaration** — that what you are hearing was made by a living human being, that the human consented to its use, that they will be paid for its use, and that no entity — company, government, platform, label, or AI system — can override that declaration without the artist's explicit, written, individually signed authorization.

The HVS™ mark is the Guild's seal. When it appears on a piece of work, it means the Guild stands behind what that mark represents. The Guild is the artists. The artists hold the seal. The seal cannot be bought, assigned, inherited, or transferred without the living artist's active consent key.

## HOW YOU JOIN

Joining the Guild is an act of declaration, not an application.

### Step 1 — Identity
You record your voice. Not a demo. A declaration — your name, your craft, your origin. This becomes the seed of your Voice Profile, the biometric foundation of your HVS™ certificate. It is stored in the NOIZY Vault, encrypted, permissioned only by your Consent Key.

### Step 2 — The Seven Never Clauses
You read, consider, and affirm the Seven Never Clauses — the constitutional protections that govern every deployment of your voice in the NOIZY ecosystem. These are not terms of service. They are conditions of the Guild itself. They cannot be waived. They cannot be modified by any platform partner, licensing client, or AI system.

#### THE SEVEN NEVER CLAUSES

1. **My voice will never be used without active, specific, revocable consent for each context of use.**
2. **My voice will never be used to spread disinformation, hate speech, or political manipulation of any kind.**
3. **My voice will never be cloned or replicated outside the boundaries I define in my Consent Key.**
4. **My voice will never be used in therapeutic or clinical contexts without additional specialized consent.**
5. **My voice will never be deployed for children's content without age-appropriate review and explicit approval.**
6. **My voice will never be trained into a model that I cannot audit, challenge, or withdraw from.**
7. **My voice will never be used after my death without a living estate holder actively managing a Voice Estate that I established during my lifetime.**

These are not aspirational. They are technically enforced. A deployment that violates any Never Clause does not fail gracefully — it does not deploy.

### Step 3 — Vault Sealing
Your Voice Profile, your Consent Key, and your signed Seven Never Clauses are combined into your HVS™ Certificate — a SHA-256 hashed, C2PA-signed, AudioSeal-watermarked record that is:

- Stored permanently in the NOIZY Vault
- Anchored to Arweave for permanent distributed redundancy
- Registered with your unique Guild identifier
- Visible in your artist dashboard in real time

From the moment the Vault seals, every deployment of your voice generates an event in your Royalty Ledger. Every event triggers a split. 75 to you. 25 to NOIZY infrastructure. Same day.

## THE CONSENT ARCHITECTURE

The Guild's technical enforcement layer is built on three interlocking systems:

### 1. The Consent Key
A cryptographic identifier held exclusively by the artist. No deployment — not a test render, not a demo clip, not an internal AI training pass — executes without a valid Consent Key signature for that specific context. The Key is not transferable. The Key cannot be held in escrow by a label, publisher, platform, or employer. The Key lives with the artist. If the artist dies, the Key passes to the named Voice Estate holder — and only that holder.

### 2. The Context Rule Layer
Consent is not binary. The Consent Key does not simply say "yes" or "no." It contains a rule set that defines:

- **Which contexts are permitted** (game audio, film dubbing, therapeutic, educational, advertising, etc.)
- **Which contexts require additional approval** (political, religious, therapeutic, age-gated)
- **Which contexts are permanently prohibited** (Never Clauses — non-negotiable, non-configurable)
- **Duration limits** — how long a license is valid before it must be renewed
- **Geographic restrictions** — where in the world the voice can be deployed
- **Platform restrictions** — which platforms are authorized to use the voice

### 3. The Provenance Ledger
Every deployment event is logged to an immutable provenance record: what was deployed, where, when, under which Consent Key version, and what royalty event was triggered. The artist sees this in real time. The estate holder sees this. Auditors see this. What no one sees without the artist's permission: the source recordings themselves.

## THE VOICE ESTATE

Death does not end a Guild member's rights. It transfers them.

Every artist designates a Voice Estate holder during registration — a person, a family trust, or a community organization that inherits governance of the Consent Key after the artist's death.

The Voice Estate holder can:

- Continue licensing approved uses of the artist's voice
- Revoke licenses that conflict with the artist's documented intent
- Contribute the artist's vocal record to the Cultural Archive
- Manage royalty distribution to named beneficiaries
- Prevent exploitation of the artist's likeness for any unauthorized purpose

The Voice Estate cannot:

- Change the artist's Seven Never Clauses
- License the voice for contexts the artist explicitly prohibited
- Transfer the estate to an entity the artist would not have authorized
- Strip attribution from the artist's work

Protection lasts 70 years from the date of death. After 70 years, unclaimed Voice Estates enter the Cultural Archive — permanently attributed, permanently non-commercial, permanently available for cultural education and preservation with full credit to the originating artist.

The Guild is not only an economic instrument. It is a cultural one.

### The Cultural Archive
Every Guild member has the option to contribute recordings, compositions, or oral traditions to the permanent Cultural Archive — a record of human sound that exists outside of commerce, permanently attributed, permanently available for cultural education, therapeutic research, and preservation purposes.

The Archive is governed by the Cultural Resurrection Engine protocols. Endangered languages, oral traditions, and at-risk musical forms are prioritized. Community consent governs communal works — no individual can license cultural knowledge that belongs to a community.

### The Cultural Intelligence Database
The Guild's collective Voice Profiles form the most culturally diverse, consented, attributed database of human vocal expression ever assembled. This database is not for sale. It is for deployment — through consent — to create AI-assisted experiences that are emotionally authentic, culturally literate, and humanly grounded.

Generic AI voices are commodity. Guild voices are premium tier — because behind each one is a human being, a story, a culture, and a signature that can be verified, traced, and compensated.
