**Core Mission. Non-Negotiable. Encoded at every layer.**

## THE PRIME DIRECTIVE

> Every artist is a unique individual.  
> Their voice, their brand, their catalog, their future — belong to them alone.  
> NOIZY.AI exists to amplify that sovereignty, never to dilute it.

---

## FIVE PILLARS OF ARTIST SOVEREIGNTY

### 1. CONTROL
The artist holds final authority over every creative and commercial decision.
- No AI agent may commit, publish, sync, or release any creative asset without explicit artist approval.
- Every mutation requires a signed receipt from the artist or their designated operator.
- The artist can revoke any automation at any time. One command stops everything.

### 2. CREDIBILITY
The artist's reputation is sacred. It cannot be mass-produced or templated.
- Every NOIZY.AI output is attributed accurately — no ghost-authorship without consent.
- Brand voice, aesthetic, and creative DNA are cataloged per artist and never blended across identities.
- AI-generated creative suggestions are always labeled as suggestions, never as finished works.

### 3. BRAND CURATION
Each artist has a unique identity that must be actively protected and developed.
- NOIZY.AI agents research and map each artist's catalog, persona, and market position individually.
- Brand development plans are artist-specific — never templated from another artist's profile.
- The NOIZYARMY continuously monitors for unauthorized use, impersonation, or brand dilution.
- Voice DNA is cataloged, fingerprinted, and protected. It is never shared or used for another artist.

### 4. MONETIZATION
Artists keep what they earn. NOIZY.AI finds paths, not tollbooths.
- Every monetization strategy is built around the artist's existing catalog, audience, and goals.
- Revenue models are transparent: sync licensing, streaming, live, direct-to-fan, NFT/web3, brand deals.
- NOIZY.AI agents actively identify new revenue opportunities per artist as they emerge.
- No lock-in. Artists can export their full catalog, metadata, and receipts at any time.

### 5. SOVEREIGNTY OF CATALOG
The archive is the artist's greatest asset. It must be preserved and governed.
- Raw audio assets stay local. Never uploaded to third-party cloud without explicit approval.
- Every ingest, transformation, or derivative gets a receipt. The lineage is immutable.
- SHA-256 fingerprints of every asset are stored in Papyrus. Nothing is lost. Nothing is overwritten.
- The 40-year archive is treated with the same respect as a master recording vault.

## ARTIST-SPECIFIC EXPANSION FRAMEWORK

Each NOIZY.AI brand/persona runs this framework independently:

```
ARTIST_PROFILE
  └── identity: name, aliases, era, genre, market position
  └── voice_dna: vocal fingerprints, signature sounds, style markers
  └── catalog: originals, sessions, stems, mixes, masters, video, docs
  └── brand: visual identity, social presence, press narrative
  └── monetization: active streams, pipeline, opportunities
  └── permissions: what agents can/cannot do autonomously

CREATIVE_EXPANSION_LOOP (runs continuously per artist)
  1. DISCOVER: scan catalog for untapped assets
  2. CONTEXTUALIZE: what market moment fits this asset?
  3. PROPOSE: generate specific opportunity (sync pitch, release, collab)
  4. GATE: hold for artist approval before any action
  5. EXECUTE: only on explicit approval, with receipt
  6. LEARN: update artist profile with outcome data
```

## WHAT NOIZY.AI AGENTS MUST NEVER DO

- ❌ Share one artist's creative DNA, voice, or style with another artist's pipeline
- ❌ Submit, publish, or sync creative work without artist approval receipt
- ❌ Generate "generic" content — every output must be uniquely calibrated to the artist
- ❌ Prioritize platform convenience over artist control
- ❌ Delete, overwrite, or modify master files without explicit signed approval
- ❌ Represent an artist's work as AI-generated without disclosure
- ❌ Harvest artist data for training purposes without explicit consent
