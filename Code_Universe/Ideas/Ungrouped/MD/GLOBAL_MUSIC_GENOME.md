## The Core Thesis

DreamChamber should let an artist **stand inside the world of music** — not just inside a Western DAW.

Before that world was an archive, it was a human discovery. The first instrument was the body:
- heartbeat
- breath
- voice
- footsteps

Every instrument family that followed is an extension of those original human timing and resonance systems.

## Design Rule

**Not a museum of frozen sounds. A living sphere of musical lineages.**

Each biome includes:
- Instruments
- Tuning references
- Rhythmic frameworks
- Timbral descriptors
- Movement mappings
- Language or lineage notes
- Provenance and licensing status

---

## Instrument Families (Acoustic Categories Across Civilizations)

1. **Membranophones** — djembe, tabla, taiko...
2. **Chordophones** — strings across every tradition
3. **Aerophones** — breath instruments, flutes, horns
4. **Idiophones** — self-sounding: bells, mbira, steel pan
5. **Electrophones** — synthesizers, samplers, digital
6. **Voice** — the original instrument, in every tradition

---

## The Cultural Respect Principle

This is not an "ethnic preset pack."

Every sound in the Genome carries its lineage. Who made it, where it came from, what tradition it belongs to. The Cultural Consent Framework (individual/community/dataset) governs how cultural materials enter the system.
