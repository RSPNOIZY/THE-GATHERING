## THE DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Before the architecture. The five principles that every governance decision must satisfy:

**1. Creator sovereignty is non-negotiable.** Every structure in this document exists to serve the creator. No governance mechanism, community structure, or royalty system may be designed to serve the platform at the creator's expense. If it conflicts, the creator wins.

**2. The mathematics must compound.** Income should grow over time without the creator doing additional work. A voice recorded in 2026 should generate more income in 2036 than it did in 2027 — not because the artist worked harder, but because the network grew and the attribution compounded.

**3. The system must be impossible to corrupt.** Not difficult to corrupt. Impossible. Every royalty mechanism is on-chain or publicly auditable. Every consent decision is creator-controlled. Every governance vote is transparent. The architecture prevents the betrayal, not just the appearance of it.

**4. The floor rises with the platform.** As NOIZY grows, the minimum creator income rises. The 75/25 split is the floor, not the ceiling. As enterprise licensing grows, as the Cultural Archive grows, as GABRIEL's capabilities grow — the value flowing to creators grows with it.

**5. No creator left behind.** The architecture must work for the artist in Lagos with 200 listeners and the artist in London with 2 million. The same protections. The same split. The same attribution. The same permanence.

## PART I — THE ROYALTY ARCHITECTURE

### The 75/25 Foundation

Every commercial use of Guild creative work splits as follows:

- **75% to the creator(s)**
- **25% to the NOIZY platform** (infrastructure, GABRIEL maintenance, Cultural Archive preservation, Healing Pool operations, NOIZYKIDZ)

This split:
- **Activates automatically at 30 days** — no application, no approval, no request
- **Is non-renegotiable** — not a starting point, not a "standard" that premium clients negotiate down from
- **Applies to every commercial use** — licensing, streaming, training data, enterprise, healthcare, gaming, film, advertising
- **Is permanent** — it does not expire, cannot be changed retroactively for existing creators, and any platform-wide change requires unanimous Guild vote on the royalty floor

### Revenue Types and Split Mechanisms

**TYPE A — Streaming / On-Demand**
*What:* Per-play royalties from Music World streams, embedded licensed audio, platform playback
*How it flows:* GABRIEL tracks every play. Royalties accumulate in the creator's dashboard. Minimum payout threshold: $10. Payout frequency: monthly automatic transfer.
*Attribution complexity:* Single artist = 75% to one account. Collaborative work = percentage split defined at registration, locked, unalterable without all parties' consent.

**TYPE B — Therapeutic / Healing Pool**
*What:* Per-session royalties when Guild audio is used in documented healing protocols
*How it flows:* Each therapeutic session generates an attribution event. The audio fingerprint (AudioSeal) identifies the source. 75% flows to the creator's account within 48 hours of session completion.
*Unique rule:* The Healing Pool is free to end users — the platform subsidizes therapeutic access from the 25% share. Creators are paid for every session regardless of whether the patient paid.

**TYPE C — Enterprise Licensing**
*What:* Annual or project-based licensing fees paid by healthcare systems, game studios, film productions, podcast networks, etc.
*How it flows:* Negotiated fee is split immediately on receipt. 75% distributed to creators whose voices/works are in the license. Distribution within the 75% is proportional to usage within the licensed period.
*Creator control:* Each creator sets their own enterprise licensing consent preferences at registration. Default is "available for all ethical uses." Creators can exclude specific industries (e.g., tobacco, weapons) through the consent settings.

**TYPE D — AI Training Data Licensing**
*What:* Fees paid by AI companies and research institutions to license the Guild dataset or individual voice models for AI training
*How it flows:* This is the highest-value royalty type. Creator earns an initial license fee plus ongoing usage royalties as the trained model generates commercial value.
*Critical rule:* No creator voice can be licensed for AI training without explicit, per-use consent. The consent granted at HVS™ registration covers therapeutic, streaming, and enterprise use — AI training requires a separate, explicit consent decision.
*Revenue model:* 75% of training license fee to creator. If model continues generating revenue (e.g., a trained voice assistant that remains in commercial use), the creator receives an ongoing 75% revenue share from that model's commercial income — indefinitely, including after the creator's death (Voice Estate rules apply).

**TYPE E — Fan Economy**
*What:* Direct fan support — Patron tiers, custom request fees, voice packs, personal interactions
*How it flows:* Fan pays the platform. 75% transfers to the creator account within 24 hours. Fan economy royalties are not subject to hold periods or recoupment — they are pure income from the day of transaction.

**TYPE F — Voice Estate (Post-Death)**
*What:* All royalty types continue for 70 years after the creator's death
*How it flows:* The creator designates a Voice Estate executor and beneficiary at registration. The executor has platform access to manage licensing consent settings. All royalties flow to the designated beneficiary account. Estate designation can be updated by the creator at any time during their lifetime.
*Default if not designated:* Royalties held in trust for seven years, then transferred to the Guild's Cultural Preservation Fund (which funds endangered tradition preservation and the NOIZYKIDZ scholarship program).

### The Compounding Mechanism

The most important feature of the royalty architecture is not the split percentage. It is the compounding.

When a creator joins the Guild in 2026, their voice is in the GABRIEL Cultural Intelligence Database. As the database grows:
- More enterprise clients join, licensing the database
- The license fees grow
- Each creator's proportional share of a growing pool grows
- A voice recorded in 2026 is worth more in 2036 than it was in 2027

This is not a theoretical benefit. It is built into the architecture:

- The enterprise licensing fee for the GABRIEL database is recalculated annually
- 75% of the total annual license fee is distributed proportionally to all creators in the database
- Proportionality is calculated by a usage-weighted index — voices that were used more in the preceding year receive a larger share
- Early founders (Founding 100) receive a permanent 1.25× usage weight as a recognition of their role in establishing the standard

**Compounding in practice:** An artist who joins in 2026 and is in 100 enterprise-licensed healing sessions that year earns royalties from those 100 sessions. In 2030, when the enterprise client base has grown 10×, the same voice (still in the database) may be in 1,000 sessions from the same registration effort. The income grows with the platform. The artist did the work once.

## PART II — THE GOVERNANCE ARCHITECTURE

### The Guild Council Structure

Three tiers. Each with specific powers, responsibilities, and accountability mechanisms.

---

**TIER 1 — THE CREATOR ASSEMBLY**

*Who:* All registered Guild members with active HVS™ Certificates
*Power:* Vote on all non-constitutional matters, elect Tier 2 representatives, initiate proposals
*Voting weight:* One vote per creator, regardless of tier, income, or tenure
*Quorum:* 15% of active members for standard votes; 30% for significant platform changes
*Decision threshold:* Simple majority (50%+1) for standard votes; two-thirds for platform-wide changes; unanimous for constitutional matters (the Seven Never Clauses and the royalty floor)

**What the Creator Assembly controls:**
- Acceptance or rejection of new enterprise partnership categories
- Platform feature prioritization (quarterly vote on the three most-requested features)
- Guild expansion into new countries or sectors
- Endorsement of legislative positions
- Election of the Guild Council (Tier 2)

---

**TIER 2 — THE GUILD COUNCIL**

*Who:* 21 elected creators, serving two-year staggered terms
*Structure:* 7 seats per region (Americas, EMEA, Asia-Pacific) to prevent geographic imbalance
*Power:* Day-to-day governance decisions, platform policy, dispute resolution, partnership approval
*Accountability:* Recall vote possible at any time with 25% of Creator Assembly signatures

**What the Guild Council controls:**
- Approval of new enterprise licensing agreements above $100K
- Dispute resolution between creators
- Platform policy within the constitutional constraints
- Budget allocation of the 25% platform share
- Guidance to the technical team on GABRIEL development priorities

---

**TIER 3 — THE CONSTITUTIONAL GUARDIANS**

*Who:* Five senior Guild members (minimum 3 years tenure), appointed by the Council with Assembly ratification
*Power:* Constitutional review only — can veto any decision that violates the Seven Never Clauses or the royalty floor
*Limitations:* No affirmative power. Cannot initiate policy. Cannot override the Assembly on non-constitutional matters. Their only function is to prevent constitutional violations.

**The Seven Never Clauses (constitutional, unanimous required to alter):**
1. NEVER train an AI on a creator's voice without explicit written consent
2. NEVER reduce the 75/25 royalty floor
3. NEVER share identifiable creator data with third parties without creator consent
4. NEVER allow a single enterprise to own more than 10% of the Guild's licensing revenue
5. NEVER prioritize platform revenue over creator welfare in any product decision
6. NEVER allow a Voice Estate to be seized by creditors or courts without due process and creator family notification
7. NEVER close the Healing Pool to free public access

### Conflict Resolution

**Creator vs. Creator disputes** (royalty attribution, sample clearance, collaborative work disagreements):
- Step 1: Automated mediation by GABRIEL — review of registration records, usage data, consent chains
- Step 2: Guild Council subcommittee review (three members, drawn by lot)
- Step 3: Full Council decision
- Step 4: Creator Assembly appeal (requires 5% of Assembly signatures to trigger)

**Creator vs. Platform disputes** (royalty calculation disputes, consent violations, certification issues):
- Step 1: Technical audit by the independent Provenance Auditor (Adam Robb/iPSS role)
- Step 2: Constitutional Guardian review if constitutional question involved
- Step 3: External arbitration (designated arbitration service, mutually agreed at platform launch)
- Platform never adjudicates its own disputes with creators. Always independent review.

### The Carrier Network (Recruitment Architecture)

Every creator who joins becomes a potential recruiter. The architecture incentivizes good-faith growth without distorting incentives toward extraction.

**The Referral Compact:**
- When Creator A recruits Creator B, Creator A receives a 0.5% "discovery bonus" on Creator B's first 24 months of royalty income
- This is additive — it comes from the platform's 25%, not from Creator B's 75%
- It expires after 24 months — the incentive is for genuine introduction, not perpetual extraction
- It is capped at 50 referrals — to prevent the creation of "referral empires" that distort the economy

**The Ambassador Tier:**
- Creators who have referred 10+ active Guild members are designated Guild Ambassadors
- Ambassadors receive a permanent seat at the Creation Council (advisory, non-voting on constitutional matters)
- Ambassadors are the first point of contact for creator concerns in their region/sector
