# Human Voice Sovereignty Charter

## Mission

Consent as executable code. Provenance as default. Revocation as sacred. Compensation as automatic.

## Non-Negotiables

- Consent is required before any voice use, synthesis, likeness binding, or identity binding.
- Revocation is sacred and must preserve historical earnings.
- Compensation is automatic.
- RSP_001 keeps the Founding Actor floor of 85/15.
- Standard creators keep the 75/25 floor.
- Raw voice, biometric, contract, and private vault material remains local unless explicitly approved for a bounded destination.

## Pilot Scope

The MC96 pilot supports up to 10 artists with:

- consent tokens,
- C2PA manifest references,
- SHA-256 asset hashes,
- royalty splits,
- auditable ledger events,
- revocation history.

## Publication Gate

No work is publishable until it has:

1. active consent,
2. C2PA manifest reference,
3. asset hash,
4. royalty split,
5. audit receipt.
