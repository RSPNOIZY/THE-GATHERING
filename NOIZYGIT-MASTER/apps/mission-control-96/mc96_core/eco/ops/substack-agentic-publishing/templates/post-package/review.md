# Review Checklist

## Editorial

- [ ] Title fits the piece.
- [ ] Opening is specific and human.
- [ ] Claims are source-backed.
- [ ] No hallucinated quotes, stats, or names.
- [ ] Ending has a clear reader takeaway.

## NOIZY Safety

- [ ] Voice consent checked.
- [ ] Music rights checked.
- [ ] Image/video rights checked.
- [ ] Child safety reviewed if relevant.
- [ ] No private keys, private links, internal secrets, or subscriber data.

## Substack

- [ ] Audience setting checked.
- [ ] Send-email setting checked.
- [ ] Tags/section checked.
- [ ] Preview link reviewed.
- [ ] Test email reviewed.
- [ ] Human publish approval recorded.
